Best advice for writing a blog

03 January 2020, by Priyanka Pal

Good blog posts answer one question well. Start there.

Pick one topic per post

Write the question your customer would type into Google, and use it as the title.

Write short

Use short sentences and short paragraphs. Add a heading every few paragraphs so readers can scan.

Finish with a next step

Tell the reader what to do next: call, message or read a related post.
